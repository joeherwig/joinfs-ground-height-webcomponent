## 0.1.0

- `JoinfsGroundHeight`: thinned-out terrain lookup along a track (Open-Meteo by default, or your own provider), interpolation,
  height above ground per frame, statistics.
- `<joinfs-ground-profile>`: zoomable, scrollable altitude / ground height diagram of one aircraft (wheel, drag, pinch, keys,
  buttons), feet or metres, light and dark, playhead, `seek` event.
- English built in, locale files for de, es, fr, it, ko, nl, pt, uk.
